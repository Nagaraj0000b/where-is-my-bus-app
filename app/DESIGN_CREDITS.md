# Design credits

Where Is My Bus borrows **arrangement and motion ideas**, not pixels, code or
copy. Every colour, typeface, size and pixel in the app is its own.

The design references below were read through the lookap registry. They are
credited here.

| Reference | Designer | What was borrowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dot Matrix Departure Board | kaya · [@kayanoesasaki](https://x.com/kayanoesasaki/status/2105807084860104738) | The departure-board idea: a dark route board, condensed capitals, tabular time numerals, quiet spacing |
| Flight Status Card Microinteraction | Michael Nowak · [@mnowakdesign](https://x.com/mnowakdesign/status/2110430533876658614) | Two endpoints, a thin route line, the vehicle in the middle |
| Minimal History Timeline Explorer | Stefan · [@heystefan_](https://x.com/heystefan_/status/...) | Fine ticks and sparse labels on the route ribbon |

The Chelaile Android teardown in `research/chelaile/reverse-engineering/` is
product research only. No Chelaile code, assets, UI strings, branding or API
endpoints are used in this app.
